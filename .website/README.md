## UnicX

Doris the Unicorn was just minding her own business when she was captured and turned into a Unicode Emoji 🦄
As if that wasn't weird enough a mad scientist Emoji took pity on her and gave her a Unicyle for a body.

#### Now she is the **Unicode Unicorn on a Unicycle!**

*(or just **UnicX** because she doesn't want her name to take up too many bytes...)*

#### A roguelike platformer with a colorful twist.

Rainbows are your weapon, but also a convenient way to travel between clouds.
Use them to move around, or as shields, or to trap enemies and destroy them.
Increase damage, rainbow length, number, thickness, casting speed and other unique upgrades to defeat waves of critters that have taken over the sky.
Falling out of the sky will cost HP.. so grab the warp upgrade, or learn to use the rainbows to stay airborne.

### Controls

#### Mouse+Keyboard

* ⬅️ / ➡️  or A/D - Move left and right
* ⬆️ or W or Space - Jump
* Shift / X / Click - Fire 🌈
* Mouse - Aim

#### Gamepad

This game is very well suited to this kind of input.
* Left stick - Move
* Right Stick - Aim
* Left Shoulder/Trigger - Jump
* Right Shoulder/Trigger/A - Fire 🌈
