A roguelike platformer with a colorful twist.
Rainbows are your weapon, but also a convenient way to travel between clouds.
Use them to move around, or as shields, or to trap enemies and destroy them.
Increase damage, rainbow length, number, thickness, casting speed and other unique upgrades to defeat waves of critters that have taken over the sky.
Falling out of the sky will cost HP.. so grab the warp upgrade if you can.
Use WASD/Cursors with Mouse, and works really well if you have Gamepad.
